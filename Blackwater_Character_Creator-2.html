Step 1 — Name & Race

Race is purely for roleplay flair and how the world sees you. It has no mechanical effect.

Step 2 — Background Profession

Your profession is RP flair that helps you fit into the world, earn coin, and interact with other players. The economy is player-driven.

Step 3 — Class

Everyone starts with 1 Life Point and chooses 4 feats/spells from their class list. Starting gear and gold differ by class.

Step 4 — Feats & Spells

Select exactly 4 feats/spells. Some have prerequisites. Combat spells can later be prepared into your 6 combat slots.
